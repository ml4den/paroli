---
name: Settings interaction design
description: User-approved direction for separating simple and advanced passphrase controls.
---

Keep one generator view with an explicit Fixed separator / Random characters choice, rather than Basic/Advanced tabs. Show the digit-count field when numbers are enabled. Put capitalization in More options.

**Why:** The user approved this recommendation to resolve confusion caused by opening Advanced options overriding the still-visible separator dropdown.

**How to apply:** Treat disclosures as visibility only. Opening or closing More options must not change generation rules or regenerate the phrase. Keep independent settings and their saved browser preferences intact when hiding controls.
